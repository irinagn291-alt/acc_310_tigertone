Tigertone is a private shelf for readers who finish a book and want one chosen line to come back on that month and day.

• Add a book on Shelf with Title, Author, Pages, and Start page
• On Reading, set the Page, write a New line, Save line, and mark Read again
• Close book, pick one line (File, then nail), then Close book so This line returns on this date
• On home, when a line is Due, tap Nail this line; tap Lift this line to take it back down
• Coming up lists Next, Later dates, and Nails
• Settings offers Show introduction, Contact, Export, and Delete all
• Lines stay on this device

For readers who want one anniversary line from a finished book, not a store catalog or rating shelf.
